# Meowball Rush v3.2.0

Implemented the six requested playtest upgrades locally. No commits or git pushes were performed.

- Ten six-act adventures with five glowing checkpoints each, varied wide/medium/precision lanes, rolling hills, early boosts and launches, and new Stage 01/03 spirals.
- World-specific close 3D borders and two layered diorama rings, with actual vertex/footprint clearance checks. Roadside frames use scenery cutaways; distant backdrops remain opaque outside the camera boom. New scenery is batched by material and area, with limits of 300 added batches and 450,000 vertices per course.
- Twelve partial mud/honey zones, each with a continuous 1.5m dry skill line. Rendering and rolling friction interpolate the same boundary on the actual floor triangles.
- Periodic spikes with 0.55s warning, 1.1s active and 1.8s safe retraction; low-rate swept contact checks. Pendulums and rolling pins appear in Stages 03–10. Mushroom, toy, barrel and cheese bumpers provide horizontal rebounds, boing audio and sparkles.
- Falls cost 8 seconds; spikes and pendulums cost 5 seconds. Floating red/gold receipts, timer flashes and distinct audio accompany penalties. Zero time immediately ends the run, before pickups or finish detection can restore time.
- Original pointer-lock steering, orbit/zoom/WASD/R controls, camera distance minima, rail sliding, moving critters and checkpoint recall are retained.
