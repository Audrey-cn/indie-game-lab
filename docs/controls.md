# Lunar Field Station field manual

[English README](../README.en.md) · [简体中文操作手册](controls.zh-CN.md)

Open the standalone HTML in a browser with WebGL 2 enabled. Select **Start expedition** to begin the full mission. **Settings** is available from the title screen and the pause menu.

## Desktop controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `W` / `↑`, `S` / `↓` | Drive forward or reverse |
| `A` / `←`, `D` / `→` | Steer left or right |
| `Space` | Brake and let the rover settle |
| `Shift` while driving forward | Boost |
| Mouse movement | Look around while the pointer is locked. Click the scene to regain camera control if needed. |
| Hold `E` near a crystal, instrument or survey station | Sample a crystal, take an instrument reading or calibrate the station when prompted |
| Tap `E` at the Earth observatory | Open the telescope after parking |
| `R` | Fabricate a kit at the workshop. At a build site, preview the footing and then press again to install. |
| `Esc` or `P` | Pause. Pausing releases the pointer. Choose **Resume mission** to return to driving. |

## Touch controls

- Push the left joystick up to drive forward, down to reverse, or sideways to steer. Release it to brake.
- Drag on empty space on the right side of the scene to look around.
- Hold **Boost** for extra thrust. Holding it with the joystick released drives forward.
- Use **Brake** to stop. From a standstill, hold **Brake** with the joystick released to reverse.
- Tap the action button that appears near a valid target to start the indicated interaction. For an installation, tap to preview the footing and tap again when the footing is valid.
- Use the pause icon to access the menu. Where the browser offers fullscreen, the game requests it on start or resume and provides a toggle in **Settings**.

## Mission sequence

1. Park near crystals and survey instruments. On the radar, solid dots mark crystals and hollow dots mark instruments. Gather **two crystal samples and two survey readings**, then park at the landing zone to upload the initial survey.
2. Park at the workshop and use `R` or tap **Fabricate**. Take the kit to a relay build site. Preview the footing, move the rover if it blocks the footprint, then start the installation. Once the arm begins placing the kit, let it finish.
3. Collect a **third crystal sample**. Fabricate the terrain survey station, deploy it at an available site and let it complete its power-on check. Park and hold `E` or tap the calibration button to record its first reading.
4. Fabricate and install the solar resupply station. Park within its charging area to top up the rover. Return to the landing zone to send the expedition log.

If the sampling prompt asks for clearance, reposition the rover and let it settle before trying again. Allow the arm to finish stowing the core before driving away. Avoid the patrol's scan beam, which costs mission time if it catches the rover.

## Optional Earth observation

Find the **lilac diamond** on the radar, park, and use `E` or tap **Observe Earth**. Drag to frame Earth, scroll or pinch to zoom and use the side arrows to switch between visible light, far-ultraviolet and Full Earth. On a keyboard, `[` and `]` switch modes, the arrow keys pan, and `Home` recenters the view at 1×.

Use **Align the telescope** when prompted, then select **Capture image** or press `E`. Review and download the simulated PNG from the capture panel. Choose **Return to driving** to close the telescope.

## Settings and guidance

Choose English or Simplified Chinese, toggle music and sound effects independently, and turn the close-up action camera on or off. The in-game **Field manual** also lets you restore the companion drone's guidance.
